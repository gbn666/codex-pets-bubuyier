# 布布 Action Prompt

Action themes used for 布布:

- idle: gentle breathing, soft blinking, slow double blink, tiny sway, subtle bounce, head tilt, brief yawn
- waving: small sleepy wave and soft nod
- jumping: cozy tiny bounce
- failed: confused head tilt, sleepy eye rubbing, head lowered, dozing off and waking softly
- waiting: holding a small text-free envelope, optionally with simple teal cap or satchel
- running: prop-free sleepy tiny run
- review: holding a small generic soda bottle, sipping, silly happy expression, no labels or logos

All rows keep the milk-tea-brown bear identity, dark outline, yellow cheek patches, and simple chibi proportions.
