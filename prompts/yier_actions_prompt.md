# 一二 Action Prompt

Action themes used for 一二:

- idle: gentle breathing, soft blinking, slow double blinking, tiny body sway, subtle bounce, curious stare, slight head tilts, hands together
- waving: small friendly wave, tiny two-handed welcome gesture
- jumping: tiny happy hop and heartwarming bounce
- failed: embarrassed blush, shy look away, lowered head, nervous sway, droopy pause, relieved recovery
- waiting: holding a small cup of noodles or warm drink
- running: prop-free tiny running and bouncing
- review: holding and checking a small blue text-free phone

All rows preserve 一二's no-eyebrow face and simple rounded identity.
